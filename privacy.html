Privacy

This is Noche's page. It has no accounts and stores nothing about you on its own.

Cookies and ads

Only if you press Accept, it loads these ad platforms' tags: Meta Pixel. They tell those platforms you visited, so Noche can measure ads and show ads to people who visited. If you press Decline, none of them load. You can change your choice with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the page.

The newsletter

There is no newsletter sign-up on this page.

Noche's own tools

Noche uses a personal app, Noche Studio, that connects only to Noche's own accounts. Its Pinterest connection reads Noche's own boards and pins, read-only, to collect inspiration on Noche's own computer. It collects no data about anyone else, posts nothing, and shares nothing.
